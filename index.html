Who We Are

A shared space for independent providers who love what they do.

Total Vibe Salon & Studios is a multi-use beauty & relaxation building in Aiken, SC, housing several independent service providers under one roof. Owner Kristin Stone built Total Vibe around a team of Lash and Hair specialists, and the building is home to a growing lineup of independent providers — each running their own craft, their own schedule, their own vibe.

  • Lash Extensions with our salon team
  • Full Service Hair & Extensions with our salon team
  • Waxing now available with Lilly Lopez
  • Nails now available with Hailey
  • Spray Tan now available with our salon team
  • Yoga from an independent studio partner, right in the building
