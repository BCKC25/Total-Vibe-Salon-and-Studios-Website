Who We Are

Independent Beauty Providers Under One Roof

Total Vibe Salon & Studios is a multi-use beauty & relaxation building in Aiken, SC, housing several independent service providers under one roof. Owner Kristin Stone built Total Vibe around a team of Lash and Hair specialists, and the building is home to a growing lineup of independent providers — each running their own craft, their own schedule, their own vibe. We're also welcoming new independent pros through booth and room rental in Aiken.
