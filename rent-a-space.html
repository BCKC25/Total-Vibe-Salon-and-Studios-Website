FAQ

Questions, answered

How long is the lease term?

12 months, with the option to renew. We're looking for long-term tenants who want to grow with us.

Is a deposit required?

Yes — a deposit equal to one month's rent is due at signing, in addition to your first month's rent.

What are the studio's access hours?

Access is as needed. Exterior doors use code locks, so you can come and go on your own schedule.

Can I bring and sell my own products?

Yes — each provider is independent and can customize their space and sell their own products. Subletting your space to someone else isn't permitted.

How soon can I start?

As soon as possible — space is available now. Contact us today and we'll get you started.
