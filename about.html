Our Story

A building built for more than one kind of self-care.

Total Vibe Salon & Studios started with one simple idea: independent beauty and wellness providers deserve a shared home that feels like more than a hallway of doors.

The Building

One roof, many independent providers.

Total Vibe Salon & Studios is a multi-use beauty & relaxation building housing several independent service providers under one roof and one brand. Rather than a single salon with a single style, Total Vibe is a curated collection of specialists — each running their own business, their own schedule, and their own craft — sharing one warm, welcoming space in Aiken, SC.

That means when you book with any provider in the building, you're booking directly with the specialist who will actually take care of you. Total Vibe is the roof; the people underneath it are the reason you'll come back.

Meet the Owner

Kristen Stone

Kristen Stone is the owner of Total Vibe Salon & Studios and performs Lash Extensions and Full Service Hair herself. She built Total Vibe to bring together the kind of providers she'd want to book with — skilled, independent, and genuinely invested in their craft — all in one building.
